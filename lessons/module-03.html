LESSON 01

Understanding Higgsfield Subscription Plans

A subscription plan defines the access, usage allowance, features and service conditions available to an account.

Subscription plan అంటే మీరు platform‌కు payment చేసినప్పుడు మీకు లభించే access, credits, models, features మరియు usage limits వివరాలు.

Types of Plans

1. Free Access

Free access allows users to explore selected features under the platform's current free-use conditions.

Free accountలో అన్ని models, resolutions లేదా generation features అందుబాటులో ఉంటాయని అనుకోకూడదు.

2. Individual Plans

Individual plans are designed for solo creators, freelancers and personal creative work.

వీటిలో plan‌ను బట్టి credits, model access, parallel generations మరియు additional features మారవచ్చు.

3. Business Plans

Business plans are designed for teams and organizations that need shared workspaces, pooled credits and collaboration controls.

Team సభ్యులు ఒకే workspaceలో పనిచేయడం, credits share చేసుకోవడం వంటి capabilities ఉండవచ్చు.

ఒక creator‌కు నెలకు కొన్ని images మాత్రమే కావాలి. మరొక creator రోజూ high-resolution videos తయారు చేయాలి. వీరిద్దరికీ ఒకే subscription అవసరం ఉండకపోవచ్చు.

Choose a plan based on actual workload, not just the highest advertised feature count.

LESSON 02

Monthly vs Annual Subscription

Monthly and annual subscriptions differ mainly in billing frequency, total commitment and sometimes promotional pricing.

Monthly planలో ప్రతి నెల payment ఉంటుంది. Annual planలో సాధారణంగా ఒక సంవత్సరం billing ఉంటుంది. Annual planలో monthly equivalent తక్కువగా కనిపించినా మొత్తం ముందస్తు payment ఎక్కువగా ఉండవచ్చు.

Factor Monthly Annual
Billing Monthly Yearly or as displayed
Commitment Shorter billing commitment Longer billing commitment
Displayed Price Monthly rate May show a lower monthly equivalent
Total Payment Check monthly checkout amount Check total annual checkout amount
Cancellation Review current cancellation terms Review annual cancellation and refund terms

How to Compare the Real Cost

Do not compare only the monthly equivalent displayed on the annual plan.

Example Calculation

Suppose a hypothetical monthly plan costs ₹1,000.

Monthly for 12 months:

₹1,000 × 12 = ₹12,000

Suppose an annual offer costs ₹9,600 upfront.

Annual payment = ₹9,600

Monthly equivalent = ₹800

In this hypothetical example, the annual plan costs less over 12 months, but requires a larger upfront payment.

These are teaching examples, not Higgsfield's actual prices. Always check the current checkout amount, taxes, renewal date and refund conditions.
LESSON 03

How Higgsfield Credits Work

Credits are usage units used by supported generation operations. The cost may vary by model, duration, resolution and selected settings.

Credits అనేవి AI generation కోసం ఉపయోగించే usage units. ప్రతి image లేదా videoకి ఒకే number of credits ఖర్చు అవుతాయని అనుకోకూడదు.

Factors That Can Affect Credit Cost

  • Selected AI model.
  • Image or video generation type.
  • Video duration.
  • Output resolution.
  • Quality or processing settings.
  • Additional features used in the workflow.

Example Credit Calculation

Suppose a hypothetical model costs 10 credits for one generation.

Available credits: 100

Cost per generation: 10 credits

Number of generations: 100 ÷ 10

Possible generations = 10

This is a simple example. Actual costs vary by the selected Higgsfield model and settings.

Credit Cycle

Subscription credits may refresh at the start of a defined billing or credit cycle. The exact refresh timing depends on the applicable plan and billing terms.

Official Higgsfield help information states that subscription credits do not normally roll over to the next credit cycle and unused subscription credits can expire.

Important Credit Terms

Term Meaning
Credit Balance Credits currently available.
Credit Consumption Credits used by an operation.
Credit Refresh New subscription credits added at a cycle reset.
Credit Pack Additional purchased usage credits, if available.
Credit Rollover Unused credits carried into another cycle, if permitted.
Do not assume that purchased credits and subscription credits follow identical expiry rules. Read the current terms for each credit type.
LESSON 04

What Does Unlimited Actually Mean?

Unlimited access can apply only to selected models, resolutions, quality levels or time periods defined by the subscription or offer.

Unlimited అంటే Higgsfieldలో ఉన్న అన్ని models‌ను ఎప్పుడైనా, ఎన్ని సార్లైనా, అన్ని resolutionsలో freeగా ఉపయోగించవచ్చని కాదు.

Different Unlimited Conditions

1. Selected Model Unlimited

Unlimited access may apply only to certain models included in your plan.

2. Time-Limited Unlimited

Some offers may provide unlimited access for a limited period, such as a promotional access window.

3. Resolution Restrictions

An unlimited offer may apply to specific resolutions or quality tiers, rather than every available output.

4. Speed and Concurrency Conditions

High system demand can lead to dynamic processing speeds or concurrency adjustments under applicable unlimited usage terms.

5. Platform Restrictions

Some unlimited or free generations may be available only through specific interfaces and not through API, MCP, CLI or other workflows.

Before Using an Unlimited Model

  1. Check whether the model is included in your plan.
  2. Check whether Unlimited is active for that model.
  3. Check its validity period.
  4. Check supported resolutions.
  5. Check any concurrency or speed conditions.
  6. Check whether it works in the interface you intend to use.
Example:

A plan may offer unlimited access to Model A for seven days but charge normal credits for Model B.

In that situation, Model A is covered by the offer. It does not mean every platform model is unlimited.

Professional Tip

Before a large generation session, check the model's Unlimited status and the displayed credit cost. Do not assume that a previous month's offer is still active.

LESSON 05

Free vs Paid Plans & Model Access

Free and paid accounts can differ in model access, available generations, credits, commercial rights and watermark conditions.

Free accountలో కొన్ని tools లేదా models మాత్రమే అందుబాటులో ఉండవచ్చు. Paid planలో అదనపు access, credits లేదా features ఉండవచ్చు.

Feature Free Access Paid Access
Model Access Selected availability Depends on plan
Monthly Credits May not include a recurring allowance Plan-dependent allowance
Generation Limits Free-use restrictions may apply Higher or different limits may apply
Unlimited Access Generally restricted by plan eligibility Selected models may qualify
Visible Watermark May be present Official help says paid-plan generations are without visible Higgsfield watermark

Why Does Model Access Differ?

Different models may require different processing resources, licensing arrangements and platform access conditions.

Therefore, a model available to one subscription tier may not be available to another.

Never purchase a plan based only on a social media screenshot. Check the model name and access conditions on your own account's current pricing and generation screen.
LESSON 06

Watermarks — Visible vs Invisible

A watermark is a visible mark or embedded identifier associated with content. Different watermark types serve different purposes.

Watermark అంటే image లేదా videoలో కనిపించే గుర్తు లేదా contentలో embeddedగా ఉండే identification data. రెండింటి మధ్య తేడా తెలుసుకోవాలి.

1. Visible Watermark

A visible watermark is a mark that can be seen directly on an image or video.

Higgsfield's current help guidance states that free-plan generations can carry a visible Higgsfield watermark, while new generations made on paid plans do not include that visible watermark.

2. Invisible Metadata or Markers

Some AI systems may embed machine-readable markers or metadata to identify AI-generated content.

These are different from visible logos or text. Upgrading a subscription does not necessarily remove invisible provenance markers.

Important Watermark Rules

  • Check the account type before generating.
  • Check the current watermark policy.
  • Paid-plan watermark benefits apply according to current official terms.
  • Previously generated free-plan files may retain their watermark.
  • Regenerating under an eligible paid plan may produce a new clean output.
  • Invisible metadata is not the same as a visible watermark.
Do not assume that a paid plan removes every kind of AI identification or provenance metadata. Also, do not assume it removes a watermark from an old file.
LESSON 07

Billing, Renewal, Cancellation & Refunds

Subscription billing defines when payments are collected, how plans renew and what happens after cancellation.

Subscription కొనుగోలు చేసిన తర్వాత payment ఎప్పుడు జరుగుతుంది, auto-renewal ఎలా పనిచేస్తుంది, cancel చేస్తే ఏమవుతుంది అనే విషయాలు తప్పనిసరిగా తెలుసుకోవాలి.

Auto-Renewal

A recurring subscription may automatically renew at the end of each billing period until cancelled.

Higgsfield's billing guidance describes automatic renewal for subscriptions. Check your account's current renewal date and terms.

How to Review Subscription Details

  1. Open your official Higgsfield account.
  2. Find account or subscription management.
  3. Review the active plan.
  4. Check the next renewal date.
  5. Check the payment method and billing details.
  6. Review available cancellation options.

Cancellation

Cancellation generally stops future renewals according to the platform's terms. Access may continue until the end of the current paid period, depending on the policy.

Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the current refund policy, purchase conditions and applicable laws.

Cancelling a subscription and receiving a refund are two different actions. Do not assume that cancellation automatically returns the amount already paid.

Taxes and Currency

A displayed price may exclude local taxes or other applicable charges. Review the final checkout amount before confirming payment.

Payment Safety

  • Use the official checkout flow.
  • Verify the final amount and currency.
  • Check the billing period.
  • Keep payment confirmations and invoices.
  • Never share card OTPs or banking passwords.
LESSON 08

Commercial Use, Licensing & Budget Planning

Commercial use means using generated content in business or revenue-generating activities. The right to use an output depends on applicable plan terms, model conditions and third-party rights.

Commercial use అంటే AIతో తయారు చేసిన content‌ను business, client projects, advertisements లేదా revenue earning కోసం ఉపయోగించడం.

Before Using Content Commercially

  • Check your subscription's commercial-use terms.
  • Check model-specific licensing conditions.
  • Check rights to reference images and uploaded files.
  • Obtain permission for third-party people, brands or assets.
  • Check client contract requirements.
  • Check platform restrictions on prohibited content.

Budget Planning

A professional creator should estimate costs before beginning a large project.

Example Project Budget

Item Planning Question
Images How many image generations are needed?
Videos How many clips and what duration?
Resolution Is 720p, 1080p or 4K required?
Revisions How many iterations may be needed?
Audio Are voice and audio tools required?
Subscription Which plan supports the complete workflow?

Professional Budget Formula

Estimated Project Cost = Subscription Cost + Additional Credit Cost + Other Production Costs.

Keep a buffer for revisions and unexpected generations. Avoid promising a client a fixed output cost until you understand the workflow and credit requirements.

KNOWLEDGE CHECK

Module 03 — Final Quiz

1. What is a subscription plan?

A plan that defines access, credits, features and usage conditions for an account.

2. What is the difference between monthly and annual billing?

Monthly billing charges on a monthly cycle, while annual billing generally covers a yearly period.

3. What are credits?

Credits are usage units consumed by supported generation operations.

4. What factors can affect credit consumption?

Model, duration, resolution and selected generation settings can affect credit cost.

5. Does Unlimited always mean every model is free?

No. Unlimited access can be restricted by model, plan, resolution, duration and validity period.

6. What is a visible watermark?

A mark that can be seen directly on an image or video.

7. What is invisible AI metadata?

Machine-readable information that may identify content as AI-generated.

8. Does upgrading always remove an old watermark?

No. Previously generated files may retain their original watermark. A new generation may be required.

9. What is auto-renewal?

Automatic renewal of a recurring subscription at the end of its billing period unless cancelled.

10. What should you check before commercial use?

Subscription conditions, model licensing, asset rights, permissions and applicable restrictions.
