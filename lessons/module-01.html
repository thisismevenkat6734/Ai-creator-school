LESSON 01

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science that focuses on creating systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) అంటే సాధారణంగా మనుషులు చేసే కొన్ని తెలివైన పనులను కంప్యూటర్లు లేదా machines ద్వారా చేయించే technology.

Examples of AI in Daily Life

  • Voice Assistants: Voice commands‌ను అర్థం చేసుకోవడం.
  • Recommendation Systems: మనకు నచ్చే videos లేదా products సూచించడం.
  • Translation: ఒక భాషను మరొక భాషలోకి మార్చడం.
  • Image Recognition: ఫోటోలోని objects‌ను గుర్తించడం.
  • Generative AI: కొత్త text, images, videos సృష్టించడం.

Important AI Terminology

Term Meaning
Artificial Intelligence Machines performing intelligent tasks.
Machine Learning Systems learning patterns from data.
Deep Learning Machine learning using multi-layer neural networks.
Neural Network A computational system inspired by connected neurons.
Algorithm A set of instructions or computational steps.
Dataset A collection of data used for training or evaluation.
Simple Example:

మీరు ఒక AI image tool‌కు ఒక dog photo చూపిస్తే, అది ఆ image‌లో dog ఉందని గుర్తించగలదు.

This is an example of image recognition, not necessarily image generation.

Check Your Understanding

AI మరియు Machine Learning ఒకటేనా?

కాదు. Artificial Intelligence అనేది పెద్ద field. Machine Learning అనేది AIలో ఒక భాగం. Machine Learning ద్వారా systems dataలోని patterns నేర్చుకుంటాయి.

LESSON 02

What is Generative AI?

Generative AI refers to AI systems that can generate new content such as text, images, audio, video and other media based on learned patterns and user instructions.

Generative AI అంటే మనం ఇచ్చిన instructions ఆధారంగా కొత్త content తయారు చేసే AI technology. ఇది text మాత్రమే కాదు, images, videos, audio వంటి content కూడా సృష్టించగలదు.

Types of Generative AI

Category Output Example Use
Text Generation Text Scripts, articles and stories
Image Generation Images Posters, artwork and product images
Video Generation Video clips Advertisements and cinematic scenes
Audio Generation Speech and audio Voice-overs and sound effects
3D Generation 3D assets Objects and virtual environments

How Generative AI Works

  1. Training Data: The model is trained using data.
  2. Learning: It learns patterns and relationships.
  3. Input: The user provides a prompt or reference.
  4. Generation: The model produces a result.
  5. Evaluation: The user checks and improves the result.

Important Terms

Generative Model: A model designed to generate new content.

Diffusion Model: A type of generative model commonly used in image and video generation. It learns to transform noisy data into structured content.

Transformer: A neural network architecture used in many modern language and multimodal models.

Multimodal AI: AI that can work with more than one type of data, such as text, images and audio.

Practical Understanding

Input:

A red sports car parked on a mountain road at sunrise.

Possible Output:

An AI-generated image representing a red sports car on a mountain road at sunrise.

Remember: AI does not guarantee that every generated result will be accurate or identical to your imagination. Prompt quality, model capabilities and settings matter.

ఈ మూడు terms‌ను ఒకటిగా అనుకోకూడదు.

LESSON 04

How an AI Creative Workflow Works

An AI creative workflow is a structured sequence of decisions and actions used to turn an idea into a finished creative output.

ఒక ఆలోచనను తీసుకొని దాన్ని final image లేదా videoగా తయారు చేసే వరకు మనం చేసే అన్ని steps‌ను AI creative workflow అంటాం.

Professional Workflow

  1. Idea Development

    ఏ content తయారు చేయాలో నిర్ణయించాలి.

  2. Concept Design

    Characters, locations, mood మరియు visual style నిర్ణయించాలి.

  3. Prompt Writing

    AIకి clear instructions ఇవ్వాలి.

  4. Model Selection

    పని అవసరానికి తగిన available model ఎంచుకోవాలి.

  5. Generation

    AI output తయారు చేయాలి.

  6. Review

    Errors, unwanted details మరియు quality పరిశీలించాలి.

  7. Editing

    అవసరమైన corrections చేయాలి.

  8. Final Export

    అవసరమైన format మరియు resolutionలో export చేయాలి.

Understanding Basic Settings

Setting Meaning
Aspect Ratio Width-to-height proportion of an image or video.
Resolution Output dimensions and pixel detail.
Duration Length of the generated video.
Seed A value that may help control generation variation in supported models.
Reference Image or other supported input used to guide generation.
Credits Platform usage units, where applicable.

Common Aspect Ratios

  • 16:9 — Landscape videos and YouTube.
  • 9:16 — Vertical videos and short-form content.
  • 1:1 — Square social media posts.
  • 4:5 — Portrait social media images.
  • 3:2 — Common photography format.

Note: These are common formats. The actual options depend on the selected tool or model.

LESSON 05

Image AI vs Video AI vs Audio AI

Different AI systems are designed for different types of media. A professional creator should understand which technology is suitable for each task.

Image, Video, Audio AI అన్నీ ఒకే పని చేయవు. ఏ పని కోసం ఏ technology ఉపయోగించాలో తెలుసుకోవడం చాలా ముఖ్యం.

1. Image AI

Image AI ద్వారా still images తయారు చేయవచ్చు లేదా existing images‌ను edit చేయవచ్చు.

Uses: Posters, product images, character concepts, thumbnails and artwork.

2. Video AI

Video AI ద్వారా moving scenes మరియు video clips generate చేయవచ్చు.

Uses: Cinematic scenes, advertisements, social media videos and creative storytelling.

3. Audio AI

Audio AI ద్వారా speech, voice-over, music లేదా sound-related content తయారు చేయవచ్చు.

Uses: Narration, dubbing, voice content and sound design.

4. 3D AI

3D AI workflows ద్వారా 3D objects, assets లేదా environments తయారు చేయడంలో సహాయం పొందవచ్చు.

Combined Production Example

Project: Create a Short Product Advertisement

  1. Image AI — Create a product concept image.
  2. Video AI — Animate the product scene.
  3. Audio AI — Create or add narration.
  4. Editing — Combine and refine the content.
  5. Export — Produce the final advertisement.

ఈ విధంగా multiple AI technologies‌ను ఒకే production workflowలో ఉపయోగించవచ్చు.

LESSON 06

AI Limitations, Ethics & Professional Mindset

AI is a powerful creative assistant, but its output requires human review, judgment and responsible use.

AI చాలా శక్తివంతమైన tool అయినప్పటికీ ప్రతి result perfect‌గా ఉండదు. Human creativity, checking మరియు editing ఇప్పటికీ చాలా ముఖ్యం.

Common AI Limitations

  • Incorrect text or spelling inside generated images.
  • Unnatural hands, faces or body proportions.
  • Inconsistent characters across multiple scenes.
  • Unexpected camera movement or object motion.
  • Incorrect physical interactions.
  • Different outputs from similar prompts.
  • Generation time, credit and model restrictions.

Responsible AI Rules

  1. Use images, voices and identities with appropriate permission.
  2. Do not impersonate people deceptively.
  3. Respect copyright and licensing conditions.
  4. Do not create misleading or harmful content.
  5. Protect private and confidential information.
  6. Review generated content before publishing.

Professional Creator Mindset

A professional AI creator does not depend only on clicking the Generate button.

A professional creator understands the concept, chooses the right model, writes clear prompts, reviews the output and improves it through iteration.

మంచి AI Creator అంటే AI tool ఉపయోగించడం తెలిసిన వ్యక్తి మాత్రమే కాదు. Creative thinking, visual storytelling, problem solving మరియు quality control తెలిసిన వ్యక్తి.
